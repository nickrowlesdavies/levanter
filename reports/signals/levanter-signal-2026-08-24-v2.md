## What the model can and cannot do

The power law is a fit of price to time. It has no economic mechanism behind it, it cannot call tops, and a line that holds in the historical sample can break out of it. It is a valuation anchor, not a timing tool. Treat the fair value and the floor as distant reference points, never as targets and never as a reason to size up.
