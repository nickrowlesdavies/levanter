## What the model can and cannot do

It is a statistical fit of price to time. It has no hard economic mechanism behind it, cannot call tops, and may fail outside the historical sample. It is a valuation anchor, not a timing tool. Treat the fair value and the floor as distant reference points, never as targets and never as a reason to size up.
